*Numbers love to line up in patterns, and once you learn to read them, you can guess what comes next every time.*

## What you do

You look at a short run of numbers, like 2, 4, 6, 8. You ask one simple question. What is the rule? Here the rule is add 2. So the next number is 10. You found the pattern, and now you can keep going as long as you like.

## What is really happening

This is real mathematics, the kind that mathematicians do every day. A run of numbers in order is called a sequence. The rule is the secret that ties them together. Sometimes you add the same amount each time. Sometimes you double, so 1 becomes 2, then 4, then 8. Sometimes the numbers are square numbers, like 1, 4, 9, 16, which come from 1 times 1, 2 times 2, 3 times 3, and so on. One famous rule is the Fibonacci pattern, where you add the two numbers before to get the next one, giving 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8. Each of these hides a rule, and finding it is the whole game.

Once you name the rule, something powerful happens. You can predict not just the next number, but every number after it, forever. That is the magic. A short pattern lets you see far into the future.

## Where it shows up

Patterns are everywhere. The days of the week repeat. A clock counts in steps. Stairs climb by the same height each time. When you save the same amount of pocket money every week, your total grows in a neat sequence. Spotting the rule is a skill you already use, even if no one gave it a name.

## The short version

- A sequence is a run of numbers with a hidden rule.
- Common rules are add, double, squares, and Fibonacci.
- Name the rule and you can predict every number that follows.
